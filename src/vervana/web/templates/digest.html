Data freshness

Getting real-time prices

Wholesale · near-live

Agmarknet wholesale prices are real and refreshed daily by the ingest pipeline (data.gov.in, GODL-India) — {{ options.qcomm|length + options.wholesale_only|length }} commodities carry a national reference. This is as live as the official feed gets.

Quick-commerce · panelled

Blinkit / Zepto / Instamart have no open, licensed price API, and scraping their apps is brittle and against their terms — so retail rows are manual panel observations ({{ options.qcomm|length }} commodities), never scraped-and-relabelled. Refresh them by importing a panel file: uv run vervana ingest qcomm-csv <file>, or type them one at a time on the price panel →.

To make the board truly live for more commodities, the honest routes are a sanctioned quick-commerce data partnership or a standing human price panel — either flows in through the same qcomm-csv importer and inherits full provenance. We won't fake a live feed.

Method

How the reference is chosen

Delhi video quotes are preferred when unit inference is plausible. Otherwise the national Agmarknet median is used. Prices are compared, never averaged.
