The Book — hold / release intelligence

Each lot you hold, with an advisory verdict on holding vs selling now: expected net realisation minus the carrying cost, every recommendation showing its evidence, assumptions, and what would invalidate it. Nothing here is executed.

⚠
Advisory only, and honest about what it doesn't know. Where a commodity's price band has not cleared the kill bar (today, all of them), the expected sale price falls back to today's price held flat and the verdict is gated to WAIT — the Book never issues a confident HOLD/RELEASE off an unvalidated forward price. Carrying-cost rates are visible assumptions (RISK[R16]/R17); freight reuses the sourcing estimate (R15). Destination riverine flood exposure is shown per lot (M19a); live gauge data is access-pending.
