Aspirational India

NITI Aayog's Aspirational Districts Programme names the {{ ov.total }} districts the Government of India has prioritised for focused development. This layer overlays that official membership onto the price and production data — because one of the programme's five themes, Agriculture & Water Resources, is exactly the supply signal this platform reasons about.

◎
Membership and framework, sourced. Scores, not yet. Everything on this page — the {{ ov.total }} districts, their states, and the five-theme framework — is the official programme definition. The live indicator scores and rankings sit behind NITI's Champions of Change dashboard, which has no open, licensed bulk feed, so this platform does not show them rather than invent them (RISK[R14]).
