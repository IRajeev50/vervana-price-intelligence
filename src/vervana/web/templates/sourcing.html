Landed-cost sourcing

The benchmark shows the cheapest mandi at source. This shows the cheapest mandi delivered to you — real wholesale ₹/kg plus an estimated freight cost — so the nearest isn't assumed cheapest and the far-but-cheap mandi isn't missed. Each mandi is a real sourcing point, linked to its price evidence.

⚠
Landed cost is an estimate (RISK[R15]). Wholesale ₹/kg is real (Agmarknet, executed_summary — never blended with retail). Freight is estimated as distance × ₹{{ '%.0f'|format(result.rate) }}/tonne-km over a state-centroid distance approximation — shown to compare mandis, not as a quoted freight rate. Within one state distance reads as ~0 (refine with mandi-level coordinates later).
